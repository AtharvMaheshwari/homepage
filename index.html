Ion Traps & Mechanical Analog - Research Project

Prof. Kantimay Das Gupta · IIT Bombay · Jan 2025 - Jul 2025
  • Studied systems that trap ions in oscillating electric fields (Paul traps), creating a 3D pseudo-potential well using electrodes of specific geometries, and applied concepts from Floquet theory and numerical analysis
  • Designed and fabricated a 3D-printed rotating-saddle mechanical analog of an ion trap, using Fusion360 software
  • Analytically determined and experimentally verified the angular velocity required for stability of a sphere on a rotating saddle
  • Experimentally achieved a bound trajectory for a sphere lasting nearly 280 seconds at optimal angular velocity
  • Derived the effective trajectory of the centre of mass for an extended object and refined the surface geometry so that the centre of mass follows the desired hyperbolic-paraboloid (saddle) path
Project Docs

Role of Nonlinear Resonances in Protection of Qubits - Research Project

Prof. Sudhir Ranjan Jain · UM-DAE CBS · Aug 2024 - Present
  • Analyzing stable elliptical and unstable hyperbolic fixed points in macroscopic quantum systems, such as Josephson junctions, to understand system dynamics under varying parameters and optimize stability
  • Studying effects of perturbations in the system based on fundamentals of the Kolmogorov-Arnold-Moser (KAM) theorem
  • Optimizing parameters to inhibit information leakage and suppress decoherence caused by quantum tunneling
Project Docs

Surface States in Periodic Potentials

Prof. Hridis Pal · Condensed Matter Physics · Aug 2025 - Nov 2025
  • Analyzed the conditions for Shockley surface states in finite 1-D periodic potentials, incorporating physically correct decaying-wavefunction boundary conditions at crystal edges
  • Differentiated Shockley and Tamm states by studying their distinct origins: band inversion versus surface potential discontinuities
  • Extended the model to three dimensions, presenting theoretical surface bands and explaining their implications for electronic conductivity in materials such as diamond and metals
Project Docs

Mass Function & Distance of Star Clusters using GIT Data

Prof. Varun Bhalerao · Astrophysics · GROWTH-India Telescope · Aug 2025 - Nov 2025
  • Procured live GIT observations of two open clusters and performed photometric reduction in the u, g, r, i bands to extract apparent magnitudes and compute stellar temperatures from color indices (g-r, g-i, u-g)
  • Estimated cluster distances and absolute magnitudes of stars by aligning the observed sequence with the standard Hertzsprung-Russell (H-R) diagram, and plotted absolute magnitude against temperature
  • Determined the Spectral Type and Luminosity Class of each star to estimate its stellar mass
  • Constructed the cluster mass function and fitted theoretical isochrones to estimate the age of the cluster
Project Docs

Electronic Circuit Equivalent of Mechanical Impacting System

Prof. Punit Parmananda · Non-Linear Dynamics · Aug 2024 - Nov 2024
  • Performed stability analysis of mechanical soft and hard impact systems and developed their corresponding electrical circuit analogs
  • Observed a narrow band of chaos between period-1 and period-2 regimes of displacement amplitudes, with bifurcations arising from variations in soft-impact distance or system energy
  • Presented the work as part of a three-member team to a class of 65 students, illustrating how chaotic behavior manifests in nonlinear systems
Project Docs

Grover's Quantum Search Algorithm - QISKIT

Prof. Siddhartha Santra · Quantum Information & Computing · Aug 2025 - Nov 2025
  • Implemented Grover's Quantum Search Algorithm for n-qubit systems (search space of size 2n) using the QISKIT framework in Python
  • Designed custom oracles and diffusion operators to detect one or more marked states in an unsorted quantum database
  • Explored practical challenges in implementing Grover's algorithm on existing quantum hardware and compared its performance with classical search methods
